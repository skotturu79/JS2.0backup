Joule Widget — Test Page

This page loads the widget with a mock agent that echoes your message and returns a fake warehouse table. Use it to verify UI behaviour without a live backend.

Keyboard shortcut: Ctrl+Shift+J to toggle the panel  |  Esc to close  |  or click the blue button in the bottom-right corner.
